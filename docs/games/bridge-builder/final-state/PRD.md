## 1. Problem statement

Grades 1–8 children need short, focused practice; LevelBest needs a free-site magnet that proves quality before family accounts exist and an earned break that releases pressure without stealing practice time. Most practice also teaches symbol manipulation without magnitude intuition — children compute but cannot see *why* quantities compose or are equivalent. Bridge Builder does both with one mechanic: filling an exactly-sized span, so success **is** composition/equivalence. Current-generation work proved the pedagogy and engine but not production feel, and human evidence gates remain open. This document settles what "done, production quality, grades 1–8, safe" means.

## 2. Target users [UPDATED]

**Learners** (mobile-first, no adult coaching mid-play):

- **Maya, 6 (g12)** — counts on fingers, reads few words: numeral + true proportional length + optional dot face, counting ticks, zero reading burden, 48 px targets, a nearly unfail-able first puzzle.
- **Dev, 8 (g34)** — partial ×tables: equal-group puzzles, factor-pair discovery, missing-addend bridges, stars for elegant builds.
- **Lena, 11 (g56)** — fractions shaky: dual-labelled fraction/decimal planks where two ¼ planks visibly span one ½, plus ratio/scale blueprints.
- **Omar, 13 (g78)** — algebra-ready, bored: variable beams, signed shims, `2x + 3 = 15` discovered because the bridge demands it, Pythagorean braces, scale drawings.

**Parent/teacher trust surface.** The parent brief itemizes game minutes inside total time (never merged into learning time), renders the learning-share split honestly (≥80 % gate shown even when it fails), and adds one curriculum line per session ("composed numbers to 20"). The free site saves nothing and says so.

**Earned-break host (lessons).** One break card after a lesson block: "Break time! Bridge Builder — 90 seconds." The countdown is a **return promise**, not a threat: the ring is visible from second one and stays **steady warm amber** — never pulsing, flashing or ticking with sound. At ≤10 s the state changes through **non-colour carriers**: the ring becomes **segmented** (one segment drops per second), the **numeric remaining time becomes visible text**, and the copy switches to "Back to practice soon"; the colour stays steady amber. At expiry the construction is auto-saved and the child is returned to practice; the **3 s auto-return is non-cancellable and non-deferrable**; the visible "Back to practice" control returns **early** only, and cannot cancel, postpone or extend. **No extension or skip control exists anywhere** `[existing]` (source: `SPRINT_READINESS.md` § earned-break controls; `UI_TEST_FINDINGS.md` break-flow entry). The crossing subject is an **abstract non-character load marker** (e.g. lantern cart / suspended crate), not a character (§3.7).

## 3. Core player loop and product experience [UPDATED]

```
setup → PUZZLE (span + exact label; tray of 5–7 true-to-length planks)
   pointer drag | tap-tap | keyboard → bounded intent → engine
   overshoot → plank overhangs the far anchor by exactly its excess, one wobble,
      signed diff chip at the overhanging end, plank returns to tray
   underfill → fills left→right, remaining span stays visibly open
   exact fit → span closes, crossing beat, stars, optional "Build it another way?"
   Check it (submit) at any time — a non-exact submit restates the difference as
      lengths (incorrectSubmit); an exact composition auto-verdicts on placement
→ free site: next puzzle … until 90 s or 6 bridges (whichever first) → SUMMARY
   (bridges · points · best streak · hints · coaching line · lantern skyline ·
   Play again / Change level / All games · "Nothing is saved — that's okay.")
→ earned break: 90 s wall-clock window → S4 RETURN ONLY (no summary, no Play again,
   no Change level, no Relaxed toggle); expiry auto-saves and returns to practice
```

**3.1 Challenge setup.** Four content-first band cards (plain-language detail, grade only as parent microcopy); auto-band chip from the placement signal, always overridable; one honest sentence on session shape; sound and **Relaxed build** toggles (the Relaxed toggle is surfaced on S0 and in the hub card's secondary line so it is discoverable without adult coaching — the disclosed untimed accessibility path pending alternate-version verification, §8); preview strip of upcoming spans and per-band skill chips. The first in-game screen is the **puzzle**, not instructions.

**3.2 Construction interaction.** Left-to-right fill of the open span; placed planks stay liftable; Undo reverses the last placement, Reset clears; preset (missing-addend) planks render grey inside the span so the numbers stay truthful. Fill order and slot occupancy are **engine outputs** (unit-based `slots[{slotIndex, offsetUnits, pieceId?}]`, `openSlots[]`, `fillOrder[]`); the renderer never derives slot geometry from pixels.

**3.3 Piece selection and manipulation.** Three equivalent paths over one **closed, frozen** intent union (`selectPiece | placePiece | removePiece | reset | submit | requestHint | continue | presentationComplete`): pointer drag with a **purely visual** magnet offset (≤12 px) that **never changes the target slot**; tap-select then tap-place (never drag-only); keyboard (arrows rove tray → slots → controls, Enter/Space places, `U` undo, `R` repeats the last announcement, Esc pauses). The target slot is the nearest engine-derived slot centre with a deterministic tie-break by lower `slotIndex` (Q-23). Focus order ascends by value even when the layout is shuffled. The ninth listed action path is **`submit` — the visible "Check it" control** (focusable; Enter/Space activates; Esc still pauses). **The intent union is exactly the frozen v1.0.0 set. Combine, split, decompose and any other new verb are out of contract**: adding one changes the legal-move set and requires a separate approved GAME issue with a contract re-version, engine legality/scoring changes and telemetry changes (§4.1). It is a v1 non-goal (§13).

**3.4 Visual explanation of composition/equivalence.** Three redundant channels per plank — value label, true proportional length (g12 ≥24 px/unit legibility floor, ≤48 px on the smallest supported viewport), grain/segment pattern; group planks carry segment dividers; a dot-face toggle supports pre-symbolic g12 play. The span prints its exact remaining value, the overhang chip its exact signed excess, and equivalence appears when two compositions span an identical distance. Counting ticks show in g12/g34, hidden from g56+.

**3.5 Invalid-placement feedback.** Failure reads as information about lengths: the plank overhangs by exactly its excess, wobbles once, carries an end-anchored diff chip, returns. No red X, buzzer, alarm transient or score penalty — overshoots cost only clock. A non-exact **`submit` renders `incorrectSubmit`**: the copy restates the exact signed difference as lengths, carries no X/buzzer, and reads as information, never as a verdict on the child; **0 penalties**. g78 signed-shim rounds include an overshoot witness plus a negative shim that repairs the construction.

**3.6 Recovery and undo.** Unlimited retries; every placement reversible by lift, Undo or Reset; stuck detection surfaces the hint affordance without forcing it; three consecutive stuck puzzles raise a neutral "Try one level easier?" chip at most **once per round**, shown mid-session, suggestion only, **never** an automatic band change; mid-session exit is clean with no guilt copy. An unsolvable tray is impossible by generator contract; if it occurred, "New planks" refills and logs `invariant_violation`.

**3.7 Completion payoff and crossing.** On exact fit the span closes, cliffs connect, **an abstract non-character load marker traverses the completed span** (decorative, post-verdict, non-authoritative), score floats near the bridge, and stars award for par-length and hint-free builds. Marker-load sag scales with inefficiency (`pieces − par`) plus a landing thud: *perceived* consequence without a physics authority. The decorative layer is **deterministic for a given `renderSeed`**, removable under reduced motion (`sag = 0`), and **may never emit intents** (Q-19). Target: next puzzle interactable ≤1.5 s Ⓟ; crossing ≤700 ms, instant under reduced motion. The payoff is the structure closing, not a popup.

**3.8 Progression and pacing.** Engine constants (single authority): `ROUND_CAP_SECONDS = 90`, `ROUND_CAP_BRIDGES = 6`, **whichever first**, all bands and both free-site/break surfaces (the break is a fixed 90 s wall-clock window). The engine owns the deadline on a **monotonic** clock (injected; `performance.now()`-based in production, fake-clock in test) and owns expiry; the host supplies visibility and route-lifecycle events as engine *inputs* on a separate channel (not a player intent; the closed union is unchanged). **The only unclocked mode in v1 is `Relaxed build`** — an in-session toggle on the free site (bridges-built progress, no clock, stuck threshold 40 s / 6 failed placements Ⓟ). **There is no endless free-build shelf in v1**: the previously proposed Workshop/Sandbox free-build mode is an **explicit non-goal** (§13) because it is owned by no child, is not evidenced as shipped anywhere in the repository ledger, and an unbounded unscored surface reachable from the earned-break host would breach the locked bounded-session constraint. Names "Workshop" and "Sandbox" are **retired names** for that non-goal (traceability only).

**3.9 Hint behaviour.** A ladder, never an answer key: **L1** restates the relationship without the solution ("Your planks make 9. The gap needs 12."); **L2** highlights one tray piece belonging to *some* valid solution; **L3** ghosts one valid placement (dashed, 50 % opacity, non-hit-testable). Hard cap L3. Hints appear only via the sparkle affordance or when the engine's stuck condition fires. **The engine constant is the single authority: 20 s with no legal progress OR 3 failed placements, whichever first; under Relaxed the time trigger is 40 s and the failure trigger is 6 Ⓟ.** No other threshold may appear in any document. Hints cost only the +2 zero-hint bonus and never break the streak.

**3.10 Replayability without manipulative engagement.** Play again; session-scoped beat-your-best (guarded `sessionStorage`, graceful no-op when blocked); a **non-blocking inline** second-construction offer on ≤40 % of solvable puzzles: the next puzzle slides in after 900–1500 ms and is immediately playable, the card is anchored beside the HUD, is never modal, its 5 s auto-dismiss is its **lifetime, not a gate**, and it is never re-offered on that puzzle; no card is offered at round end or inside the earned break. The engine owns the second-construction bonus (+5). No energy system, winback timer, daily-streak pressure, leaderboard, child-vs-child comparison, or any path that extends the break window.

**3.11 Responsive phone / tablet / desktop.** Phone portrait primary; tablet and desktop reuse the identical structure at scale. Single-column reflow at 200 % zoom with nothing clipped; only the bridge pans (as one unit) and it **never scales below the legibility floor**; one visual language across pointer/touch/keyboard; targets ≥48×48 px (tray planks ≥56 px tall, ≥8 px spacing) while plank visuals keep the ≥24 px/unit geometry via separate hit layers, with hit-area expansion capped (≤±12 px beyond visual bounds) and nearest-centre prioritisation. DPR ≤3 supported; internal render scale capped at 2; parity asserted at DPR 1/2/3. Real-device qualification is `[required work]` in GAME-134.

**3.12 Entering the earned break, the pause rule and the return contract (written once).** Tab-blur **pauses the clock on the free site only**, against a cumulative pause budget of **60 s per round**; once the budget is exhausted the round ends at the next resume and the summary is shown honestly. Inside the earned break the window is **wall-clock**: blur does not stop the deadline (the pause copy says so: "Paused — the break clock keeps running"), and an expired window returns the child to practice on resume. Device sleep is treated as hidden. If the deadline passed while hidden, the engine **expires deterministically** (break: save-at-expiry then return; free site: end the round and show the summary honestly). **No path extends a break**: no control, DOM mutation, query parameter or reload may restore a window. The visible "Back to practice" control returns early only.

## 4. Educational goals [UPDATED]

**4.1 Authority rule (frozen).** A deterministic TypeScript engine is the **sole** authority for piece lengths, composition, legal pieces, exact fit, correctness, scoring, progression, curriculum behaviour, **session deadline and expiry**. Lengths are scaled integers: each piece carries `units`, each puzzle a `denominator`, labels derive from `formatScaled(units, denominator)`, and a bridge fits **iff** `compositionUnits === gapUnits` — no floating-point epsilon in the verdict path. Renderers own pixels only: `widthPx = units * unitPx` is one-way; pointer coordinates choose *which* piece and *which* intent but never carry a length. Collision, physics, pixel overlap, snapping distance and animation are forbidden as correctness inputs; the magnet offset is visual-only and cannot change the target slot; decorative post-verdict physics is allowed only if deterministic, seeded, non-authoritative, intent-silent and removable under reduced motion. No renderer-derived geometry may appear in the verdict or legality path. Any change to equality, solvability, scoring or legal actions needs a separate approved GAME issue (BB-CONTRACT-1 is additive only and changes no authority semantics).

**4.2 Curriculum coverage — 14 CCSS-tagged skills** (catalog: `src/lib/bridgeBuilder/skills.ts`; catalogue of record owned by **BB-CONTENT-1**):

- **g12, grades 1–2:** compose-10 (1.OA.C.6, 1.OA.D.8); compose-20 (1.OA.D.8, 2.OA.B.2); missing-addend (2.NBT.B.5).
- **g34, grades 3–4:** factor-pairs (3.OA.A.4, 3.OA.C.7, 4.OA.A.4); mult-groups (3.OA.A.1, 3.OA.A.4, 3.OA.C.7); fraction-equiv (3.NF.A.3, 4.NF.A.1); decimals-tenths (4.NF.C.5, 4.NF.C.6).
- **g56, grades 5–6:** unlike-denom (5.NF.A.1); decimal-ops (5.NBT.B.7); ratio-scale (6.RP.A.3); linear-eq (6.EE.B.5, 6.EE.B.6).
- **g78, grades 7–8:** rational-eq (7.EE.B.4, 7.NS.A.1d); scale-drawing (7.G.A.1); pythagorean-span (8.G.B.7).

**Coverage object (makes "14/14 reachable" falsifiable):** supply floor per **skill × band × difficulty tier** ≥ **12 distinct solvable puzzles per cell Ⓟ**, asserted by a generator property test (not by reading `skills.ts`). **Generator invariants:** ≥1 exact solution per puzzle (property-tested per skill and seed); "another way" puzzles guarantee ≥2 **geometrically distinct** solutions, where two solutions are geometrically distinct **iff** their multiset-of-interval decompositions (piece lengths and slot offsets, in units) differ; decoys are structurally honest — the named `decoyHonesty` property: every decoy either keeps the puzzle solvable or overshoots by more than the largest plank; the entry band asserts ≥45 % multi-solution supply Ⓟ; scaled integers stay below 2³¹. Stale inherited codes (a conditional `3.MD.D.8`, a future `6.G.A.1`) are removed and the epic text is reconciled to the shipped 14-skill catalogue — a content/jira-admin edit under **BB-CONTENT-1**, not product engineering.

**4.3 Difficulty scaling from the placement signal.** `PlacementResult` maps grade → band (≤2 → g12, 3–4 → g34, 5–6 → g56, 7–8 → g78) with the placement *third* shifting within-band sub-difficulty; domain strengths (number-operations, fractions-ratios, geometry-measurement, algebraic-thinking) bias which skills are pulled first, so a fractions growth-area pulls fraction and unlike-denominator skills forward. Band locks for the round (predictability over churn) and only the within-band easier/harder knob moves content, so a misconception never silently promotes a child mid-session.

**4.4 Measurable learning outcome.** Session-level, privacy-safe, no per-child profile on the free site. **Definitions:** a **round** = one bounded instance (90 s / 6 bridges; Relaxed = 6 constructions); a **session** = one tab session containing ≥2 rounds or ≥12 puzzles. **Signals and sample rules:** **hint-free solve rate** (solves with `hintLevelMax = 0`) and **overhang→fit recovery rate** (overshoots followed by an exact fit within two placements) are computed **per session with a minimum sample of 12 puzzles**; below that minimum no trend is reported. A within-session rise may be reported **only** as a descriptive comparison at n ≥ 12, with **no causal claim**. **Second-construction acceptance** = offered puzzles solved with a genuinely different (geometrically distinct) composition. **Skill coverage** = distinct skills per round and complete 14-skill rotation achievable within a session (≥12 puzzles). **Honesty guard** = zero `invariant_violation`; game minutes excluded from learning share; split reported even when it fails. These are practice signals, **not efficacy evidence**: no causal claim is made, no pre/post instrument is needed, and any efficacy claim requires a study that does not exist (new, unapproved scope).

**Why Poly Bridge 3 is kept and scoped.** It is the right reference for construction interaction (tactility, readable bridge state, cause-and-effect, satisfying completion, responsive transitions). **Why World of Goo 2 is demoted (approved O-1 rationale).** Its contributions (responsive direct manipulation, cohesive audiovisual personality) are already required by GAME-133/GAME-171 and mapped to Q-06/Q-10; its distinguishing value is authored expressive personality (creature characters, bespoke music, hand-built worlds) — the investment this product deliberately does not make — and it sharpens no numeric-readability dimension. ## 7. Privacy, safety and engagement policy [UPDATED]

**No PII, ever:** no child identifiers, accounts, names or birthdates on the free site; telemetry is session-scoped and passes a typed `validateEvent` guard rejecting PII-shaped payloads; the parent brief consumes aggregates only. **Storage:** session-scoped (`sessionStorage`) with explicit "clears when you close the tab" copy and a **graceful no-op when blocked** (the no-op must be asserted in tests; a blocked storage API may not change visible behaviour); cross-visit persistence waits for family accounts (non-goal here). **Engagement is hard-blocked:** no ads, loot boxes, paid game content, cosmetic economy, FOMO/urgency timers, loss-aversion framing, playtime leaderboards, child-vs-child competition, infinite-play loops, or anything punishing a day off; streaks reward accuracy only, are immune to hints, reset only at session end, are framed informationally, and use a stacked-stones construction badge. **Prompts never read as obligations:** bonuses are additive, decline copy is neutral ("Nice — keep building"), and the card auto-dismisses without being re-offered. **The earned break is bounded by construction:** fixed 90 s **wall-clock** window (blur does not stop the deadline; the pause copy discloses this), no extension affordance, saved-not-lost at expiry, forced **non-cancellable** return. **The free site's pause is bounded:** tab-blur pauses the clock against a cumulative **60 s budget per round**; once exhausted, the round ends at the next resume and the summary is shown honestly. **Child-safe defaults:** audio low and toggleable, no external links, no UGC or chat, no share-to-social on the play surface; **CSP `script-src 'self'` with no third-party scripts, no inline eval, restricted `frame-ancestors`, SRI/hash verification for every CDN-delivered asset, asset hashes in the build manifest, and no third-party analytics on the play surface**. Current-generation storage and network privacy audits passed `[existing]` (source: `SPRINT_READINESS.md` privacy/telemetry entry); they must be re-run on the next-generation build `[required work]`.

**Cross-visit metrics are not claimed.** Because the posture forbids cross-visit identity, returning visitors, day-2 voluntary return and share/waitlist conversion are **not computable and not claimed** until family accounts ship (non-goal here); no share or waitlist surface is designed in v1 (§10).

## 8. Accessibility requirements [UPDATED]

**Standard: WCAG 2.2 AA, targeted and assessed per success criterion.** The fixed-clock surface does not support a blanket claim.
